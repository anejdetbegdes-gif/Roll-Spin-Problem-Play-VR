### Notes for the team
- Results must come from **real participants** only. Export the responses to Excel (Responses → Open in Excel) and send them to Claude to write the "Demo and User Feedback" section of the report.
- The assignment asks for a demo with **3–5 participants**; the showcase may give more. Report the real number.
- Participants are adults (university students/visitors). Do not let children use the headset at the showcase unless allowed by Meta's age rules, the school's policy and a parent/guardian.
- Wipe the headset face cushion between players; keep sessions short (about 10 minutes); stop if anyone feels dizzy.
