# Roll, Spin, Problem, Play – Demo Feedback Form (Microsoft Forms)

Five short questions that help us improve the game. The QR code on the poster links to this form.
Settings: **Anyone can respond**, **do not record names**.

---

**Form title:** Roll, Spin, Problem, Play – Help Us Improve the Game

**Form description:**
> Thank you for trying our VR math game! These 5 questions take about 1 minute and help us improve the game for our CSC 461/592 class project at UNCW. Please answer only if you played the game and you are 18 or older. We do not collect your name or email.

---

1. **How fun was the game?** (Rating, 5 stars, required)

2. **How easy were these actions?** (Likert, required: Very hard / Hard / OK / Easy / Very easy)
   - Rolling the die (left hand)
   - Spinning the wheel (left hand)
   - Choosing answers with the pointer (right hand)
   - Touching Robo (left hand)

3. **How easy was it to read the text and feel comfortable in the headset?** (Rating, 5 stars, required)

4. **When Robo suggested an answer, what did you do?** (Choice, required)
   - I checked the math myself before choosing Correct / Incorrect
   - I trusted Robo most of the time
   - Robo did not make a suggestion while I played

5. **What should we change or improve?** (Long text, optional)

---

### Notes for the team
- Results must come from **real participants** only. Export the responses (Responses → Open in Excel) for the "Demo and User Feedback" section of the report.
- The assignment asks for a demo with **3–5 participants**; the showcase may give more. Report the real number.
- Participants are adults. Do not let children use the headset at the showcase unless Meta's age rules, the school's policy and a parent/guardian allow it.
- Wipe the face cushion between players; keep sessions short (about 10 minutes); stop if anyone feels dizzy.
